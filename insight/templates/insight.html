How It Works

1. Configure a Marina client
Enter the client_id of a querying stream registered with Marina. The chatbot will only see tables that stream allows.
2. Provide the private key
Paste the private key generated when you created the client. It is used to mint OAuth bearer tokens against Marina.
3. Ask questions
The LLM picks the right table, Marina fetches the data, and the LLM answers in plain English.
